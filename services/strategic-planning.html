Strategic Planning for Online Business

Strategic Planning

Data-backed strategies built around your goals — channel selection, budget allocation, and a roadmap for sustainable growth.

Overview

Many businesses jump into marketing without a clear plan, spreading their budget across channels and hoping something works. Strategic planning fixes that. It gives you a data-backed roadmap so every dollar is invested intentionally and every channel supports your goals.

I work with you to understand your business, market, competitors, and objectives. From there I build a comprehensive marketing strategy — the right channels, messaging, budgets, and timelines — designed for sustainable, measurable growth.

Whether you're launching a new online business or scaling an established one, a clear strategy reduces wasted spend, aligns your team, and gives you confidence in every marketing decision.

What's Included

Market & Competitor Analysis

Understand your market position, audience, and what your competitors are doing so you can stand out.

Channel & Attribution Planning

Choose the right marketing channels and understand how they work together to drive conversions.

Budget & ROI Forecasting

Allocate your budget strategically and forecast the returns you can realistically expect.

Roadmap & Action Plan

A clear, phased roadmap with priorities, timelines, and the KPIs that will measure your success.
