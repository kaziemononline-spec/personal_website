Canva Design Services

Eye-catching visuals for your brand

Overview

Great design makes your brand stand out — but not everyone has the time or skills to create professional-looking visuals. I design custom marketing materials using Canva that are polished, on-brand, and ready to publish across your social media, website, or print channels.

From social media posts and story templates to promotional banners, flyers, and presentation decks — I handle a wide range of design needs. Every design is tailored to your brand colors, fonts, and style guidelines so your visual identity stays consistent across every piece of content.

The best part? Since I work in Canva, you get editable templates that you can tweak yourself anytime. No expensive software, no steep learning curve — just clean, effective design delivered fast.

What's Included

Social Media Post and Story Designs

Custom-designed posts, stories, and carousels for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and other platforms — sized perfectly for each channel.

Promotional Posters and Banners

Attention-grabbing posters for promotions, events, sales, or announcements. Suitable for both digital and print use.

Brand-Consistent Visual Templates

Editable Canva templates set up with your brand guidelines, so you can create future posts yourself while maintaining visual consistency.

Fast Turnaround on Design Requests

Most single-design requests are delivered within 24–48 hours. Bulk orders are scoped with a clear timeline upfront.

What's NOT Included

Logo or Brand Identity Creation

This service is for creating marketing materials using existing brand assets. I do not design logos or build brand identities from scratch.

Video Editing or Animation

Video production, motion graphics, and complex animations are not part of this service. I focus on static visual design.

Advanced Photoshop or Illustrator Work

All designs are created in Canva. If you need advanced raster or vector work done in Adobe tools, this service may not be the right fit.

Print Production or Shipping

I deliver digital files only. Printing, production, and physical delivery are your responsibility.

How It Works

1

Submit a Brief

Tell me what you need — type of design, dimensions, brand colors, text/content, reference images, and any specific requirements.

2

First Draft

I create the initial design in Canva and share a preview with you within the agreed turnaround time.

3

Revisions

You review and request changes. I offer up to 2 rounds of revisions per design to make sure it's exactly what you want.

4

Final Delivery

Once approved, I send you the final files in your preferred format (PNG, JPG, PDF, or editable Canva template link).

Who This Is For

This service is ideal for:

  • Small business owners who need regular social media graphics but don't have design skills
  • Entrepreneurs launching a new product or service and needing promotional materials fast
  • Content creators who want polished, brand-consistent visuals without hiring a full-time designer
  • Anyone who wants editable Canva templates they can reuse again and again
