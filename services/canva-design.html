Visuals That Make Your Brand Stand Out

In a world where people scroll past content in seconds, your visuals need to stop them in their tracks. I create polished, professional Canva designs that capture attention, communicate your message clearly, and reflect your brand identity.

What This Service Includes

Social Media Post & Story Designs

Instagram posts and stories, Facebook cover images and post graphics, LinkedIn banners, and Twitter headers — all sized correctly for each platform and styled to match your brand.

Promotional Posters & Banners

Campaign posters, sale announcements, event banners, and promotional graphics for both digital and print use — designed to drive attention and action.

Brand-Consistent Visual Templates

I create reusable Canva templates with your brand colors, fonts, and logo locked in, so you or your team can produce consistent on-brand content anytime.

Marketing Collateral

Flyers, brochures, one-pagers, business cards, email headers, and digital ad creatives — all designed to maintain a cohesive brand experience across every touchpoint.

Presentation Deck Design

Clean, professional slide decks for pitches, proposals, webinars, and internal meetings — with consistent branding, data visualization, and engaging layouts.

Fast Turnaround & Revisions

Most design requests are delivered within 24-48 hours, and I include revisions to ensure every piece meets your expectations before final delivery.

My Process for This Service

1

Brief & Brand Review

I review your brand guidelines, existing visuals, and design requirements to understand your style, audience, and the purpose of each design piece.

2

Concept & Draft Creation

I create initial design concepts in Canva, applying your brand colors, fonts, and imagery to produce drafts that align with your vision and marketing goals.

3

Review & Refine

I share the drafts with you for feedback, then make revisions until every detail meets your expectations — colors, layout, messaging, and overall impact.

4

Final Delivery

I export the final designs in the required formats (PNG, JPG, PDF, or editable Canva templates) and organize them for easy access and future use.
