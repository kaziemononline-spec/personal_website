Facebook Ads

High-performing Facebook ad campaigns engineered for real results — targeting, creative, and budget optimization for maximum ROAS.

Overview

Facebook and Instagram ads remain one of the most powerful ways to reach new customers, retarget warm audiences, and scale your business — but only when they're set up correctly. I plan, create, and manage campaigns that are built to convert, not just get likes or views.

Every campaign starts with understanding your goals, your target audience, and what makes your offer compelling. From there, I design the ad strategy, write the copy, create or source visuals, set up precise audience targeting, and manage your budget to get the best possible return on every dollar spent.

I also handle ongoing optimization — testing different audiences, creatives, and placements — so your campaigns keep improving over time. You get regular performance reports so you always know exactly how your ad spend is performing.

What's Included

Campaign Strategy & Audience Targeting

Detailed audience research and segmentation, plus a campaign structure designed to reach the right people at the right stage of their buying journey.

Ad Creative & Copywriting

Engaging ad copy and visual concepts tailored to your brand voice and audience, including text, image selection, and basic creative direction.

Budget Optimization for Maximum ROI

Strategic budget allocation across campaigns and ad sets, with ongoing adjustments to shift spend toward the best-performing combinations.

Performance Tracking & Reporting

Weekly or bi-weekly reports showing reach, engagement, conversions, cost per result, and ROAS so you see exactly where your money is going.

How It Works

1

Discovery & Goal Setting

We discuss your business, audience, offer, budget, and campaign objectives (leads, sales, traffic, awareness).

2

Audience Research

I research your ideal customer profile, build custom audiences, and identify lookalike or interest-based targeting opportunities.

3

Creative Development

I write ad copy, select or create visuals, and design the ad structure (carousel, single image, video) for each campaign.

4

Campaign Launch

I set up and launch campaigns in your ad account, ensuring proper tracking (pixel, conversions API) is in place.

5

Monitoring & Optimization

I monitor performance daily and adjust bids, budgets, audiences, and creatives to improve results and reduce wasted spend.

6

Reporting

You receive clear, regular reports showing campaign performance, key insights, and recommendations for the next period.
