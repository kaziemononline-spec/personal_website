Facebook Ads That Convert

High-performing ad campaigns that deliver real results

Overview

Facebook and Instagram ads remain one of the most powerful ways to reach new customers, retarget warm audiences, and scale your business — but only when they're set up correctly. I plan, create, and manage ad campaigns that are built to convert, not just get likes or views.

Every campaign starts with understanding your goals, your target audience, and what makes your offer compelling. From there, I design the ad strategy, write the copy, create or source visuals, set up precise audience targeting, and manage your budget to get the best possible return on every dollar spent.

I also handle ongoing optimization — testing different audiences, creatives, and placements — so your campaigns keep improving over time. You get regular performance reports so you always know exactly how your ad spend is performing.

What's Included

Campaign Strategy and Audience Targeting

Detailed audience research and segmentation, plus a campaign structure designed to reach the right people at the right stage of their buying journey.

Ad Creative and Copywriting

Engaging ad copy and visual concepts tailored to your brand voice and audience. I handle text, image selection, and basic creative direction.

Budget Optimization for Maximum ROI

Strategic budget allocation across campaigns and ad sets, with ongoing adjustments to shift spend toward the best-performing combinations.

Performance Tracking and Reporting

Weekly or bi-weekly reports showing key metrics — reach, engagement, conversions, cost per result, and ROAS — so you see exactly where your money is going.

What's NOT Included

Website Development

Building or redesigning landing pages, websites, or funnels is not included. I can recommend tools or developers if you need one.

Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

This service covers paid ads only. Organic search optimization is a separate discipline and not part of ad campaign management.

Professional Graphic Design

While I create basic ad visuals and concepts, full-scale graphic design or branding packages are not included. See my Canva Design Services for custom graphics.

Ad Account Setup from Scratch

If you don't have a Facebook Business Manager or ad account yet, I can guide you through setup, but the initial configuration is your responsibility.

How It Works

1

Discovery & Goal Setting

We discuss your business, target audience, offer, budget, and campaign objectives (leads, sales, traffic, brand awareness).

2

Audience Research

I research your ideal customer profile, build custom audiences, and identify lookalike or interest-based targeting opportunities.

3

Creative Development

I write ad copy, select or create visuals, and design the ad structure (carousel, single image, video, etc.) for each campaign.

4

Campaign Launch

I set up and launch the campaigns in your ad account, ensuring proper tracking (pixel, conversions API) is in place.

5

Monitoring & Optimization

I monitor performance daily, adjust bids, budgets, audiences, and creatives to improve results and reduce wasted spend.

6

Reporting

You receive clear, regular reports showing campaign performance, key insights, and recommendations for the next period.

Who This Is For

This service is ideal for:

  • E-commerce stores and online businesses ready to scale through paid traffic
  • Local service businesses (restaurants, clinics, salons, etc.) wanting to attract nearby customers
  • Coaches, consultants, and course creators looking to generate leads and enrollments
  • Businesses that have tried Facebook ads on their own but didn't get the results they wanted
