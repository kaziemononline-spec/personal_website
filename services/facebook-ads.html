Turn Your Ad Budget Into Predictable Revenue

Running Facebook ads without a strategy is like throwing money into a fire and hoping it keeps you warm. I design, build, and manage ad campaigns that are structured for one thing: measurable results.

From audience research and ad creative to budget management and performance reporting, I handle every part of the campaign lifecycle — so you get the best possible return on every dollar spent.

What This Service Includes

Campaign Strategy & Goal Setting

I define clear campaign objectives aligned with your business goals — whether that's lead generation, sales, website traffic, or brand awareness — and build a strategy to achieve them.

Audience Research & Targeting

I build precise audience segments using demographics, interests, behaviors, lookalikes, and custom audiences from your website visitors and customer lists.

Ad Creative & Copywriting

I create compelling ad visuals, headlines, and copy tailored to each audience segment and campaign stage — crafted to stop the scroll and drive action.

Budget Optimization & Bid Management

I manage your daily and lifetime budgets, use cost-cap and bid-cap strategies, and continuously shift spend toward the best-performing ad sets to maximize ROI.

A/B Testing & Performance Analysis

I run split tests on audiences, creatives, placements, and formats to identify winning combinations, then scale what works and kill what doesn't.

Performance Tracking & Reporting

You receive clear, regular reports with key metrics — CTR, CPC, CPM, CPA, ROAS — along with insights and recommendations for continuous improvement.

My Process for This Service

1

Audience Research

I analyze your ideal customer profile, build precise audience segments using demographics, interests, behaviors, and lookalikes, and identify the best targeting approach for your campaign goals.

2

Creative Testing

I design multiple ad variations — different visuals, headlines, and copy — and run structured split tests to identify which combinations drive the strongest engagement and conversions.

3

Campaign Launch & Optimization

I deploy the winning ad sets with optimized budgets, bids, and placements, then monitor performance daily — adjusting targeting, spend, and creatives to maximize ROI.

4

Reporting & Scaling

You receive clear weekly reports with key metrics (CTR, CPC, CPA, ROAS), and I scale the best-performing campaigns while continuously testing new angles for sustained growth.
