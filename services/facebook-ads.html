Overview

Running Facebook ads without a strategy is like throwing money into a fire and hoping it keeps you warm. The reality is that Meta's advertising platform is incredibly powerful when used correctly, but it's also complex — audience targeting, creative formats, bidding strategies, placement optimization, and the ever-changing algorithm all need to work together to deliver results.

I design, build, and manage ad campaigns that are structured for one thing: measurable results. From audience research and ad creative to budget management and performance reporting, I handle every part of the campaign lifecycle so you get the best possible return on every dollar spent. This service is for business owners who want predictable, trackable growth from their social media advertising investment.

What sets this approach apart is the combination of data-driven strategy and creative execution. I don't just launch ads and hope for the best — I research your audience, test multiple creative angles, optimize based on real performance data, and scale what works. The result is a campaign that improves over time rather than burning out, giving you sustainable customer acquisition at a cost you can count on.

What's Included

Campaign Strategy & Goal Setting

I define clear campaign objectives aligned with your business goals — whether that's lead generation, online sales, website traffic, or brand awareness — and build a strategy designed specifically to achieve those outcomes within your budget.

Audience Research & Targeting

I build precise audience segments using demographics, interests, behaviors, lookalike audiences, and custom audiences from your website visitors and customer lists. The better your targeting, the lower your cost per result.

Ad Creative & Copywriting

I create compelling ad visuals, headlines, and copy tailored to each audience segment and campaign stage — crafted to stop the scroll, communicate your value proposition, and drive action.

Budget Optimization & Bid Management

I manage your daily and lifetime budgets using cost-cap and bid-cap strategies, continuously shifting spend toward the best-performing ad sets to maximize return on ad spend.

A/B Testing & Performance Analysis

I run structured split tests on audiences, creatives, placements, and formats to identify winning combinations, then scale what works and eliminate what doesn't — ensuring your budget is always spent on the highest-performing variations.

Performance Tracking & Reporting

You receive clear, regular reports with key metrics — click-through rate, cost per click, cost per mille, cost per acquisition, and return on ad spend — along with insights and actionable recommendations for continuous improvement.

My Process

Every campaign begins with audience research, where I analyze your ideal customer profile, build precise audience segments using demographics, interests, behaviors, and lookalikes, and identify the best targeting approach for your specific campaign goals. Next comes creative testing — I design multiple ad variations with different visuals, headlines, and copy variations, then run structured split tests to identify which combinations drive the strongest engagement and conversions.

Once winning creatives are identified, I move to campaign launch and optimization, deploying the best-performing ad sets with optimized budgets, bids, and placements. I monitor performance daily, adjusting targeting, spend, and creatives to maximize ROI. The final phase is reporting and scaling — you receive clear weekly reports with key metrics, and I scale the best-performing campaigns while continuously testing new angles to sustain long-term growth.
