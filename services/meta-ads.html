Meta Ads Expert

Full-funnel Facebook & Instagram ad management — from audience research to creative testing, budget optimization, and scaling.

Turn Ad Spend into Predictable Revenue

Running Meta ads isn't about boosting posts or hoping for the best. It's about building a structured, data-driven advertising system that consistently puts your offer in front of the right people at the right time with the right message.

I manage the complete lifecycle of your Facebook and Instagram ad campaigns — from pixel setup and audience building to creative testing, bid management, and full-funnel scaling. Every decision is backed by real performance data, not gut feelings.

What This Service Includes

Facebook Pixel & Conversions API Setup

I install and configure your Meta pixel, set up standard and custom events, and integrate the Conversions API (CAPI) for accurate tracking across browsers and devices.

Audience Research & Custom Targeting

I build detailed audience stacks — core demographics, interest-based segments, lookalike audiences from your best customers, and custom audiences from website visitors, video viewers, and lead lists.

Campaign Structure & Funnel Design

I architect campaigns using the PROSPECT → RETARGET → CONVERT funnel model, with separate ad sets for cold traffic, warm engagement, and high-intent audiences so budget flows to what works.

Ad Creative Strategy & Copywriting

I develop ad concepts — headlines, primary text, CTA variations, and visual direction — optimized for each stage of the funnel. I also design simple ad creatives in Canva when needed.

A/B Split Testing Framework

I run structured split tests on audiences, creatives, placements, ad formats (single image vs. video vs. carousel), and bidding strategies to identify winning combinations with statistical confidence.

Budget Management & Bid Optimization

I manage daily and lifetime budgets, use cost cap and bid cap strategies, and shift spend toward best-performing ad sets. I also use Advantage+ and dynamic creative optimization where relevant.

Retargeting & Retention Campaigns

I set up multi-layer retargeting funnels — website visitors, add-to-cart abandoners, video viewers, and past purchasers — with tailored offers that bring people back and close the sale.

Performance Dashboards & Weekly Reporting

I track CTR, CPC, CPM, CPA, ROAS, and frequency in real time. You receive weekly reports with clear insights, trends, and actionable recommendations — not just raw numbers.

Why Choose This Service

Data-Driven ROAS, Not Guesswork

Every dollar is allocated based on real performance data. I kill underperforming ads fast and scale winners aggressively so your budget works harder.

Hyper-Targeted Audiences

No more broad, wasteful targeting. I build precise audience segments so your ads reach people most likely to buy — not just those who'll scroll past.

Full-Funnel Approach

Cold traffic gets awareness ads, warm audiences get engagement ads, and hot leads get conversion offers. Every stage of the customer journey is covered.

Scalable Growth System

Once we find a profitable ad-auction combination, I scale it systematically — increasing budgets gradually, expanding audiences via lookalikes, and replicating the formula across new offers.

How It Works

1

Audit & Discovery

I review your current ad account (if any), business goals, target customer profile, past performance data, and budget to identify what's working and what needs to change.

2

Account Setup & Tracking

I configure your pixel, CAPI, custom conversions, and event tracking. Then I build audience segments and design the campaign structure with clear objectives for each ad set.

3

Creative Production & Launch

I develop ad copy and visual concepts, set up A/B tests, and launch campaigns across Facebook and Instagram with proper tracking parameters and UTMs.

4

Daily Monitoring & Optimization

I monitor performance daily — adjusting bids, pausing low-performing ads, reallocating budget, and refreshing creatives before ad fatigue sets in.

5

Reporting & Scaling

You get weekly performance reports with clear metrics. Once we hit consistent profitability, I scale the system — expanding audiences, testing new offers, and growing ad spend safely.
