Facebook & Instagram Ads That Deliver Results

Running ads on Meta platforms is more than just boosting posts. It takes strategic planning, precise audience targeting, compelling creatives, and continuous optimization to turn ad spend into revenue.

I manage the full lifecycle of your Meta ad campaigns — from strategy and setup to monitoring and scaling — so you get the best possible return on every dollar spent.

What I Do

Campaign Strategy & Setup

I design ad campaigns aligned with your business goals — whether it's lead generation, sales, brand awareness, or engagement.

Audience Research & Targeting

I identify and build custom audiences based on demographics, interests, behaviors, and lookalike models to reach the right people.

Ad Creative Guidance

I work with you to develop ad copy, images, and videos that capture attention and drive action. I also design ad visuals when needed.

A/B Testing & Optimization

I continuously test different audiences, creatives, placements, and bidding strategies to find the best-performing combinations.

Performance Tracking & Reporting

I monitor key metrics (CTR, CPC, CPA, ROAS) daily and provide clear reports so you always know how your campaigns are performing.

Retargeting Campaigns

I set up retargeting funnels to re-engage website visitors and past leads, maximizing conversions from warm audiences.
