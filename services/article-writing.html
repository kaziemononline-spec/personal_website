Article Writing

Content That Ranks on Google and Resonates with Readers

Content is the bridge between what your audience is searching for and what your business offers. But not just any content — it has to be strategically researched, thoughtfully structured, and written in a way that both search engines and humans find valuable.

I write content that does both. Every piece starts with keyword research and competitor analysis, is structured for readability and SEO, and is written in a clear, engaging tone that builds trust and guides readers toward taking action.

SEO Blog Articles & Long-Form Content

Keyword Research & Topic Clustering

I research high-value keywords using SEO tools, group them into topic clusters, and recommend a content roadmap that builds topical authority and improves domain ranking over time.

Website & Landing Page Copywriting

Clear, benefit-driven copy for your homepage, about page, service pages, and landing pages — optimized for conversion with compelling headlines, social proof, and strong calls to action.

Product & Service Descriptions

Persuasive descriptions that highlight features, translate them into customer benefits, address pain points, and overcome objections — designed to increase inquiries and sales.

Email Sequences & Newsletter Content

Welcome sequences, nurture campaigns, promotional emails, and regular newsletters that engage subscribers, deliver value, and drive repeat business.

Social Media & Ad Copy

Short-form copy for social media posts, Facebook/Instagram ad headlines and primary text, LinkedIn articles, and Twitter threads — crafted to stop scrolling and spark engagement.

Content Repurposing & Optimization

I can repurpose existing content (turn a blog post into an email series, a video transcript into an article, etc.) or refresh outdated content to improve its search performance.

Content Strategy & Editorial Calendar

How It Works

1

Topic & Keyword Research

I research your industry, competitors, and target audience to identify high-value topics and keywords. I present a content brief with the angle, target keyword, and outline for your approval.

2

Content Outline Approval

Before writing, I share a detailed outline with headings, subheadings, key points, and SEO elements (target keyword placement, internal linking suggestions). You approve the direction.

3

Writing & On-Page SEO

I write the content with SEO best practices baked in — proper heading tags, keyword density, readability score, meta title and description, image alt text, and internal/external links.

4

Review, Revise & Publish

You review the draft, I make revisions, and you receive the final content ready for publication. I can also upload it directly to your CMS if needed (WordPress, Webflow, etc.).
