Digital Marketing Advisory

Strategic guidance to grow your business online

Overview

Most business owners know they need to be online, but few know exactly where to start, which channels matter most for their specific industry, or how to connect everything into a system that consistently brings in customers. The digital landscape is crowded with options — social media, search engines, email marketing, paid advertising, content creation — and choosing the wrong path wastes time and money.

I help you cut through the noise. Through personalized consulting sessions, I evaluate your current position, identify the highest-impact opportunities, and build a clear, actionable digital marketing roadmap tailored to your business goals, budget, and capacity. This service is for business owners who want expert direction without the fluff — a practical strategy that tells you exactly what to do, in what order, and how to measure success.

The value I deliver is clarity and confidence. Instead of guessing which platform to invest in or wondering why your current efforts aren't paying off, you get a focused plan built around your unique business realities. Whether you're a startup founder launching your first campaign or an established business owner digitizing your operations, this advisory gives you the strategic foundation to grow sustainably.

What's Included

Digital Presence Audit

I review your current online footprint — website, social media profiles, Google Business listing, reviews, and existing marketing efforts — to identify strengths, gaps, and quick wins. This audit forms the baseline for every recommendation that follows.

Channel Strategy & Platform Selection

Not every platform is right for every business. I help you identify the two or three channels that will give you the best return for your time and budget, whether that's social media, search, email, paid ads, or a combination that fits your specific industry.

Audience Identification & Positioning

I help you define your ideal customer profile, understand their online behavior, and position your brand to attract them with the right message at the right time. Clear positioning makes every marketing dollar work harder.

Actionable Growth Roadmap

You receive a step-by-step plan with prioritized actions, timeline, budget recommendations, and measurable milestones. This isn't generic advice — it's a roadmap built specifically for your business, designed for practical execution from day one.

Tool Stack Recommendations

I recommend the right tools for your needs — CRM, email marketing, scheduling, analytics, design, and ad management — so you don't waste money on software you don't need and can integrate everything into a streamlined workflow.

Ongoing Consultation & Support

As you implement the plan, I'm available for follow-up calls, strategy adjustments, and answering questions. Digital marketing changes fast, and I help you stay on track as your business evolves and new opportunities arise.

My Process

I start every engagement with a thorough discovery and digital audit, reviewing your current online presence to identify what's working, what's missing, and where the biggest opportunities lie. From there, I move into strategy development, defining the right digital channels, target audience, brand positioning, and high-impact tactics aligned with your specific business goals and budget.

Once the strategy is clear, I build an actionable roadmap — a step-by-step plan with timelines, milestones, and budget recommendations designed for practical execution. As you begin implementing, I provide ongoing support and adjustment, staying available for follow-up calls, strategy refinements, and answering questions as your business evolves. This four-phase approach ensures you never feel lost or overwhelmed; you always know what to do next and why it matters.

Ideal For

This service is designed for startups and early-stage businesses launching their digital presence and needing a clear strategy to acquire their first customers without wasting resources on the wrong channels. It's equally valuable for established businesses with offline operations who want to build or improve their online presence and reach customers where they spend their time.

Businesses stuck at a growth plateau will find this particularly useful — if you've tried a few marketing tactics but aren't seeing consistent results, I help diagnose what's not working and build a strategy that actually moves the needle. And for busy owners who need direction, this service gives you a clear plan so you can execute with confidence or hand it off to a team, knowing every action is aligned with your goals.
