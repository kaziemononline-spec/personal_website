Digital Marketing Advisory

Strategic guidance to grow your business online

Strategic guidance to help you navigate the digital landscape and build a growth plan that works for your unique business.

Clarity Before Growth

Most business owners know they need to be online, but few know exactly where to start, which channels matter most for their specific industry, or how to connect everything into a system that consistently brings in customers.

I help you cut through the noise. Through personalized consulting sessions, I evaluate your current position, identify the highest-impact opportunities, and build a clear, actionable digital marketing roadmap tailored to your business goals, budget, and capacity.

What This Service Includes

Digital Presence Audit

I review your current online footprint — website, social media profiles, Google Business listing, reviews, and existing marketing efforts — to identify strengths, gaps, and quick wins.

Channel Strategy & Platform Selection

Not every platform is right for every business. I help you identify the 2-3 channels (social media, search, email, paid ads, etc.) that will give you the best return for your time and budget.

Audience Identification & Positioning

I help you define your ideal customer profile, understand their online behavior, and position your brand to attract them with the right message at the right time.

Actionable Growth Roadmap

You receive a step-by-step plan with prioritized actions, timeline, budget recommendations, and measurable milestones — no generic advice, just what's relevant to your business.

Tool Stack Recommendations

I recommend the right tools for your needs — CRM, email marketing, scheduling, analytics, design, and ad management — so you don't waste money on software you don't need.

Ongoing Consultation & Support

As you implement the plan, I'm available for follow-up calls, strategy adjustments, and answering questions. Digital marketing changes fast, and I help you stay on track.

My Process for This Service

1

Discovery & Digital Audit

I start by reviewing your current online presence — website, social media, listings, and past marketing efforts — to identify what's working, what's missing, and where the biggest opportunities are.

2

Strategy Development

Based on the audit, I define the right digital channels, target audience, brand positioning, and high-impact tactics that align with your specific business goals and budget.

3

Actionable Roadmap Creation

You receive a clear, prioritized step-by-step plan with timelines, milestones, and budget recommendations — designed for practical execution, not just theory.

4

Ongoing Support & Adjustment

As you implement the roadmap, I stay available for follow-up calls, strategy refinements, and answering questions as your business evolves and new opportunities arise.
