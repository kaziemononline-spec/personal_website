Digital Marketing Advisory

Strategic guidance to grow your business online

Overview

Running a business today means you need more than just a website or a social media page — you need a clear, actionable digital strategy. I help business owners understand how to navigate the digital landscape, identify the right channels for their specific audience, and build a roadmap that leads to sustainable growth.

Whether you're launching a new brand, trying to get your first online customers, or looking to expand your current digital presence, my advisory service gives you the clarity and direction you need. We start by understanding where you are now, where you want to be, and what's standing in the way — then build a step-by-step plan to bridge that gap.

This is not a one-size-fits-all template. Every recommendation I provide is tailored to your business model, budget, industry, and goals. You get honest, practical advice — no fluff, no unnecessary tools or tactics.

What's Included

Digital Growth Strategy Tailored to Your Business

A customized strategy document outlining the digital channels, tools, and tactics that will drive the most impact for your specific business.

Identifying the Right Platforms and Audience for Your Brand

Analysis of where your target customers spend their time online and which platforms (Meta, Google, LinkedIn, etc.) make sense for reaching them.

Actionable Roadmap to Increase Reach and Sales

A phased, timeline-based plan with clear milestones — from quick wins to long-term growth initiatives — so you know exactly what to do and when.

Ongoing Guidance and Consultation

Continued support via scheduled check-ins where you can ask questions, review progress, and adjust your strategy as your business evolves.

What's NOT Included

Hands-On Execution or Implementation

This service provides strategic advice and a roadmap, not actual execution. Running ad campaigns, designing graphics, or building websites is not included.

Ad Management Services

If you need someone to set up and manage Facebook or Instagram ads, please see my Facebook Ads That Convert service.

Design Work

Custom designs for social media, banners, or branding materials are not included. Check out my Canva Design Services for that.

Content or Copywriting

Writing website copy, blog posts, or ad copy is outside the scope of this advisory package.

How It Works

1

Discovery Call

We hop on a 30-minute call where you tell me about your business, current challenges, goals, and what you've tried so far.

2

Audit & Analysis

I review your current digital presence — website, social media, ads (if any), and competitors — to identify gaps and opportunities.

3

Strategy Development

Based on my findings, I build a personalized digital growth strategy with channel recommendations, audience insights, and a prioritized roadmap.

4

Presentation & Delivery

I present the strategy to you via a detailed document and a walkthrough call, explaining each recommendation and answering your questions.

5

Ongoing Consultation

As you implement the roadmap, I remain available for follow-up calls and guidance to help you stay on track and adapt as needed.

Who This Is For

This service is ideal for:

  • Small business owners who know they need to grow online but aren't sure where to start
  • Startup founders looking to build a digital presence from the ground up
  • Established local businesses wanting to expand their reach beyond their current customer base
  • Anyone who feels overwhelmed by the number of digital tools, platforms, and tactics available
