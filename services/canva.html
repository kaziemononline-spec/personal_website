Canva Expert

Professional brand identity design, social media graphics, presentation decks, and custom Canva templates — all tailored to your brand.

Visual Branding That Commands Attention

Your brand's visual identity is often the first thing potential customers notice. If it looks inconsistent or amateur, they'll assume the same about your product or service. I create polished, professional Canva designs that make your business look credible, memorable, and trustworthy — across every platform.

From complete brand identity systems to individual social posts and presentation decks, every design is built with purpose, consistency, and your brand's unique personality in mind.

What This Service Includes

Brand Identity Kit

Logo design (primary, secondary, and icon variations), brand color palette, typography system, and brand usage guidelines — everything you need for a cohesive visual presence.

Social Media Graphics Package

Instagram posts and stories, Facebook cover images and post graphics, LinkedIn banner and article headers, Twitter/X headers, and YouTube thumbnails — all sized and formatted per platform specs.

Presentation Deck Design

Slide decks for investor pitches, client proposals, webinars, team meetings, and conference talks. Clean layouts, data visualization (charts/graphs), and consistent branding throughout.

Marketing & Sales Collateral

Flyers, brochures, one-pagers, business cards, email headers, digital ads, and promotional banners — print-ready or digital-optimized depending on your needs.

Customizable Canva Templates

I build branded Canva templates with locked brand assets (fonts, colors, logos) and editable fields so you or your team can create on-brand content independently.

Product & E-Commerce Graphics

Product listing images, Etsy/Amazon store banners, promotional coupons, and social media product showcases designed to drive clicks and sales.

Animated Social Graphics

Using Canva's animation tools, I create subtle motion graphics for social posts and stories — GIF-style animations that grab attention without requiring video editing skills.

Why Choose This Service

Consistent Brand Identity

Every design follows the same brand guidelines — colors, fonts, and visual tone. Your audience will recognize your brand instantly across every touchpoint.

Fast Turnaround, No Back-and-Forth

Canva enables rapid design and iteration. Most projects are delivered within 24-48 hours with unlimited minor revisions so you get exactly what you need.

Editable Templates for Your Team

With custom Canva templates, your team can create new on-brand graphics anytime without needing a designer — saving you time and money long-term.

Premium Look, Affordable Price

Canva-based design delivers professional-quality visuals at a fraction of the cost of traditional graphic design tools and software.

How It Works

1

Brand Discovery Session

I learn about your business, target audience, brand personality, and visual preferences. You share any existing brand assets, inspiration links, or examples of styles you like.

2

Concept Development

I create 2-3 initial design concepts for the primary asset (e.g., logo or social media template). You choose the direction you prefer, and I refine it.

3

Full Asset Production

Once the brand direction is approved, I produce all remaining assets — social graphics, templates, collateral — maintaining full visual consistency throughout.

4

Review, Revise & Deliver

You review the complete package, I make revisions until everything is perfect, and you receive final files in your required formats (PNG, PDF, JPG, MP4 for animations).
