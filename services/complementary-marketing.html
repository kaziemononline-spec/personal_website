Complementary Marketing Services

Everything else your brand needs to grow

Overview

Not every marketing need fits neatly into a predefined package. Sometimes you need help with content planning, social media management guidance, brand messaging, or a custom project that doesn't fall under ads, design, or pure strategy. That's where Complementary Marketing Services comes in.

This flexible offering covers the gaps — the smaller but essential pieces that complete your digital presence. Whether it's setting up a content calendar, brainstorming campaign ideas, refining your brand voice, or getting advice on a specific marketing challenge, I provide targeted support tailored to what you actually need.

Think of this as your on-demand marketing assistant. You tell me what you're working on, and I provide the expertise, templates, frameworks, or hands-on help to move things forward.

What's Included

Social Media Content Planning

Help with content calendars, post scheduling ideas, and topic brainstorming to keep your social channels active and engaging.

Brand Positioning Guidance

Advice on how to position your brand in the market, differentiate from competitors, and communicate your unique value clearly.

General Digital Marketing Support

Answers to your marketing questions, reviews of your current efforts, and actionable recommendations to improve performance.

Custom Solutions Based on Your Needs

Anything else that doesn't fit into my core services — if it's marketing-related and I can help, I'll build a custom approach for you.

What's NOT Included

Full Ad Campaign Management

Ongoing Facebook/Instagram ad management is not included here. See my Facebook Ads That Convert service for dedicated campaign management.

Design Services

Custom graphic design, social media visuals, or Canva templates are not covered. Check out my Canva Design Services for that.

Web Development

Building or modifying websites, landing pages, or technical SEO work is outside the scope of this service.

Long-Term Retainer Commitments

This service is designed for flexible, project-based or hourly support. If you need a month-to-month retainer, we can discuss that separately.

How It Works

1

Tell Me What You Need

Describe your project, challenge, or goal. It can be as simple as "I need a content plan for next month" or "Help me review my current marketing approach."

2

Scope & Quote

I review your request and provide a clear scope of work, timeline, and fixed price or hourly estimate — no surprises.

3

Execution

I deliver the work — whether it's a strategy document, content calendar, brand review, or hands-on support.

4

Review & Adjust

You review the deliverable. If needed, I make adjustments until you're satisfied. Then we decide on next steps.

Who This Is For

This service is ideal for:

  • Business owners who need occasional marketing support but not a full-service retainer
  • Startups looking for guidance on content strategy, social media, or brand messaging
  • Existing clients who need something outside my core service packages
  • Anyone with a specific marketing challenge who wants expert input without a long-term commitment
