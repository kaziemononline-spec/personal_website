# QA Report — Kazi Emon Website (Fluid/Responsive Review)

**Date:** July 12, 2026
**Rating:** 9/10
**Verdict:** KEEP ✅

## What's Working
- Fluid container (`min(1400px, 92vw)`) uses available width perfectly at all sizes
- Clamp-based typography scales smoothly from 360px to 1920px+ with no abrupt jumps
- Auto-fit grids (services, articles, process) auto-adjust column count — no breakpoints needed
- Only 2 structural media queries remain (hamburger at 768px, hero collapse at 1024px)
- All sections verified: header, hero, about, services, testimonials, articles, process, contact, footer
- "Dhaka, Bangladesh" present, business hours removed, social icons visible in both contact section and footer

## Minor Issues
1. Button touch targets ~41-42px on mobile (slightly under 44px WCAG recommendation)
2. Contact page form row (1fr 1fr) is tight on narrow phones
3. Testimonials section uses inline styles bypassing the fluid CSS system

## Suggestion
Increase button vertical padding min from 10px to 14px for better mobile touch targets.
