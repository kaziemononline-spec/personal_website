## Issues Found ⚠️

### Medium Priority

1. **OG image aspect ratio is non-standard** — The OG image is 2100x2160 (nearly square). Social sharing platforms expect 1200x630 (1.91:1). This means shared links on Facebook/LinkedIn will show a cropped or oddly sized preview. Fix: Resize the OG image to 1200x630.

2. **Contact form on contact.html lacks `action` attribute fallback** — The index page form has `action="https://formsubmit.co/ajax/kaziaremon@gmail.com" method="POST"` as a non-JS fallback, but the contact.html form omits these attributes. If JS fails to load, the form silently does nothing. Fix: Add the same `action` and `method` to contact.html's form.

### Low Priority

3. **Footer "Email Me" button only on index page** — The about.html and contact.html footers lack the gradient "Email Me" CTA button that appears on the homepage footer. For consistency, it should appear on all pages.

4. **Article pages use a simplified header** — Article pages have a minimal header (just logo + "All Articles" link) without the full nav menu. This is a deliberate design choice but may disorient users who want to navigate to other sections. Consider adding at least a hamburger menu with full nav on article pages.

5. **Inline styles in testimonials section** — The testimonials section uses inline `style` attributes (`style="display:flex;justify-content:center;max-width:550px"`) rather than CSS classes. Minor inconsistency with the otherwise clean codebase.

6. **No visible focus indicators on interactive elements** — Tab-navigating the site doesn't show clear focus rings on buttons, links, or form inputs (beyond the skip link). This hurts keyboard accessibility.

7. **Testimonial placeholders** — While the honest placeholder text is fine, consider adding at least 1-2 real testimonials or case studies to build social proof for new visitors.
