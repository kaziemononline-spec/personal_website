#1 Priority Suggestion: Populate testimonial section with real social proof

#### Rating
7.5/10

#### What's Working
- Dark theme with purple/cyan accents implemented beautifully and consistently across all pages
- Skip-to-content accessibility links present on every page
- Clean, professional copywriting across all service pages with clear value propositions
- All internal navigation links use correct relative paths and work properly
- Responsive design with breakpoints at 1200px, 1024px, 768px, 480px
- Service detail pages are well-structured (hero, features, benefits, workflow, CTA)
- Consistent header, footer, and social links across the entire site
- No frameworks — fast-loading vanilla HTML/CSS

#### Issues Found
1. **Testimonials section shows "Coming Soon"** — This is the weakest part of the site. Visitors expect social proof before hiring. Even one real testimonial would be better than a placeholder.
2. **Stats display "0"** — Raw HTML shows "0 Years Experience", "0 Projects Done", "0 Happy Clients" (animated via JS, but shows zero without it). The About page claims "5 years" experience — the stats should at least match that.
3. **Article pages likely 404** — The three article links point to pages (e.g., articles/5-proven-strategies-to-boost-conversion-rates.html) that probably don't exist yet. Clicking them will return Cloudflare 404 pages.
4. **Contact form has no visible backend** — The form submits to what appears to be a JS handler. If the JS isn't wired to a real endpoint (Formspree, EmailJS, etc.), messages are lost.
5. **No meta description tags** — None of the pages include meta description tags for SEO. This hurts search result click-through rates.
6. **Missing favicon** — No visible favicon was detected, which looks unprofessional in browser tabs.

#### Suggestions
1. Add at least 1-2 real testimonials immediately (even from past work or beta clients). Remove "Coming Soon" or move it to a secondary position.
2. Fix the "0 Years Experience" stat to show "5+" to match the About page content.
3. Create the article pages or remove the links until they exist. Broken links hurt credibility.
4. Wire the contact form to a real email service (Formspree, EmailJS, or a simple PHP/mailto fallback).
5. Add meta description tags to every page for SEO.
6. Add a favicon (a simple purple/cyan icon matching the brand).

#### Verdict
IMPROVE
