## Tester QA Review — kaziemon.online

### Rating: 8.5 / 10

---

### What's Working Well

- All assets load successfully — hero image, profile photo, article images, OG image, favicon.svg, favicon.ico, favicon PNGs, and apple-touch-icon all return 200
- All three article pages exist with genuine, well-written content
- Contact form is functional — uses FormSubmit.co to POST submissions
- Strong SEO foundation — Schema.org Person JSON-LD, OG/Twitter meta tags, sitemap.xml, canonical URL, robots.txt
- Clean, professional dark theme (#030014 bg) with purple + cyan accents
- Good accessibility — skip link, semantic HTML5 landmarks, aria-labels, alt text
- Responsive design with breakpoints at 1200px, 1024px, 768px, 480px
- Smooth UX touches — scroll animations, sticky nav with scroll spy, counter animation, back-to-top, hamburger menu

### What Could Be Improved

- Hero image has loading="lazy" — should be eager or removed for above-the-fold
- Stat counters start at "0" briefly before animation fires
- No explicit width/height on img tags — can cause CLS
- OG image is nearly square (2100x2160) — 1200x630 preferred
- Sitemap not referenced in robots.txt
- No unique meta description on article subpages

### Verdict: KEEP

### #1 Priority Suggestion
Remove loading="lazy" from the hero section's img tag so the largest above-the-fold asset loads immediately, improving Largest Contentful Paint (LCP).
