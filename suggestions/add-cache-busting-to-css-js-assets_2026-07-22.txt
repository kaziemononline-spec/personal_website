- All 3 case studies visible at once — huge UX improvement

- Text feedback now shows on subscribe: "You're subscribed!" in green

**✅ Performance Wins**
- No jQuery, no Slick — faster page loads

**⚠️ Newsletter success green contrast** — `#00C851` could be brighter

## Verdict
